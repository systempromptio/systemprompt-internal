Why it matters Integrations How to use

The work behind the answers

Astound Digital builds commerce experiences for the world's most ambitious brands. This assistant puts the client relationships, pipeline, and project history behind that work one question away.

Why teams switch it on

The context for a client conversation used to take hours to assemble. Now it takes one question. That changes how a commercial team works, and what the business keeps when people move on.

Answers in seconds, not hours

Client history, pipeline, comparable case studies, and relationship notes surface the moment you ask. Conversations happen faster, with more credibility, and with the full picture in hand.

Knowledge that stays with the business

When people leave, their account history and client context usually leave with them. Connected to your own systems, that knowledge stays searchable and usable for whoever picks up the account next.

New joiners productive in days

A new Account Manager can ask for the full history of a client and get it. Onboarding becomes a structured briefing on real data instead of weeks of piecing context together.

Governed by design

Every request passes a four-stage governance pipeline and lands in a full audit trail. Changes are confirmed with you before they happen, and your data stays in your own infrastructure.

Sign in once. Connected to everything.

Log in with your existing Salesforce account and the gateway signs you into every connected source behind it. The complexity of the connections is owned by the platform, not by you.

Salesforce

Live pipeline, accounts, contacts, leads, cases, and activities. Read and write, with confirmation before every change.

Google Drive

Project documents, case studies, and capability materials, searchable in the same conversation as your CRM data.

Email

Read-only access to the conversations where relationship context lives, scoped deliberately and governed like everything else.

Claude Cowork & Claude Code

The skills arrive inside the AI tools you already use, with dashboards and artifacts pre-installed and ready on day one.

How to use it

From your work email to your first answered question in a few minutes. No config files, no tokens, no training course.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your @astounddigital.com or @astoundcommerce.com email and register a passkey. No passwords.

  2. 2

    Download the desktop bridge

    The bridge runs in your menu bar or system tray. It signs you in, installs your workspace, and keeps your skills in sync inside Claude Code, Cowork, and Codex.

    Download forMac Download forWindows
  3. 3

    Connect your AI tools

    Open your dashboard, click Connect, and follow the one-step instructions. Signing in with Salesforce connects every source behind it.

  4. 4

    Ask your first question

    Try "Give me a full briefing on my biggest account", "What is in my pipeline this quarter?", or "Draft a proposal in our voice". The skills are already there.
