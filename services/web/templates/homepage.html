One platform, your everyday AI tools

Astound's internal AI platform connects the tools you already use — Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop — to Astound's skills, governance, and identity.

  1. ✓

    A skills marketplace, out of the box

    Consultancy workflows (RFP responses, statements of work, project estimates) and brand tools (voice and tone, client emails, boilerplate) are ready the moment you sign in — no setup, no prompt engineering. Browse the catalogue.

  2. ✓

    Your Cowork profile, installed by the bridge

    The desktop bridge app signs you in, installs your personal Cowork profile, and keeps your skills, agents, and MCP servers in sync inside Claude Code and Codex.

  3. ✓

    Governed and audited

    Every AI request and tool call runs through Astound's governance pipeline — scoped, secret-scanned, and fully audited — so you can use AI on client work with confidence.

Get started in four steps

From your work email to Astound skills inside your AI tools in a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your @astounddigital.com or @astoundcommerce.com email and register a passkey — no passwords.

    Create account Sign in
  2. 2

    Download the desktop bridge

    The bridge runs in your menu bar or system tray. It handles credentials, installs your Cowork profile, and syncs Astound skills into your AI tools.

    Download for Mac Download for Windows
  3. 3

    Connect Claude Code or Codex

    Open your dashboard, click Connect, and follow the one-step instructions. The bridge links your identity to your tools — no config files, no tokens to paste.

    Open dashboard
  4. 4

    Use Astound skills

    Ask Claude Code to draft an RFP response, build a statement of work, or apply Astound's brand voice — the skills are already there.
