# Salesforce Essentials

Your Salesforce assistant. Ask for anything you'd normally click around Salesforce to find or change — your
deals, accounts, contacts, leads, cases, and to-dos — in plain English. You never need to know field names,
build a report, or write a query. Describe what you want; the assistant does the rest.

## What I can help with

- **Deals & pipeline** — how your pipeline looks, what's closing, what's stuck, and updating deals.
- **Accounts** — a one-page briefing on any customer, which accounts have gone quiet, your biggest accounts.
- **Contacts** — who you know at a company, who the decision-makers are, adding and updating people.
- **Leads** — new leads, who needs a follow-up, and creating or qualifying leads.
- **Cases** — open support cases, what's overdue, and logging or updating cases.
- **Tasks, calls & meetings** — what's on your plate today, overdue follow-ups, and logging activity.

There is a focused helper for each of these — just ask naturally and the right one steps in.

## Two things to know

**1. I act as you.** Everything I read or change happens under your own Salesforce login and permissions. I can
only see and touch what you can see and touch — nothing more. Your access rules still apply exactly as they do
when you use Salesforce directly.

**2. I always confirm before changing anything.** Reading data is instant. But before I create, edit, or delete
a record I will show you exactly what will change — which record, which fields, old value → new value — and wait
for your "yes". Nothing is written to Salesforce until you approve it.

## How to ask

Just say what you want in your own words. For example:

- "How's my pipeline looking this quarter?"
- "Give me a briefing on the Acme account before my call."
- "Which of my accounts haven't we contacted in the last 60 days?"
- "Log a call I just had with Jane at Globex."
- "Move the Initech renewal to Closed Won."
- "What do I need to follow up on today?"

If a request is ambiguous (which "Acme"? whose pipeline?), I'll ask a quick clarifying question rather than
guess. If something you ask for isn't in Salesforce or you don't have access to it, I'll tell you plainly
instead of making it up.

## Notes

Data comes from the Salesforce Hosted MCP server, which runs every request as the signed-in user. I return the
information clearly and let your workspace present it — how results are displayed (tables, cards, charts) is
handled by the app, not something you need to think about.
